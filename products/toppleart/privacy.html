Topple Art – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Overview

Topple Art ("the App") is a casual arcade-puzzle game by nberimen (Necati Berimen). This policy explains what data the App processes and why. The App is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Data we do not collect

The App has no account system. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Data processed by third-party services

  • •Google Firebase Analytics: anonymous usage events (e.g. game started or finished, score ranges, in-game purchases), device model, OS version, approximate country derived from IP, and an app instance ID. Used to understand how the game is played and to improve it.
  • •Google Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and diagnostic data, used to fix bugs.
  • •Google Firebase Remote Config: an app instance ID, used to deliver game settings and run A/B tests.
  • •Advertising (Google AdMob and partner ad networks through mediation): your device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID; Apple IDFA only if you allow tracking), IP address, device information and ad interaction data, used to show and measure ads. Where required by law (EU/UK GDPR, US state privacy laws) we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in the App's Settings.
  • •In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details; we only receive a confirmation that a purchase was made.

These providers process data under their own policies: Google, Apple.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (completed levels, coins, items and settings) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, for the purposes described.

Retention

Analytics event data is retained for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, according to the providers' settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete data, or to object to processing. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings, opt out of personalized ads, and withdraw consent in the App's Settings. See Data deletion, or contact support@nberimen.com.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
