Topple Art – Data Deletion

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Data on your device

All game progress is stored locally. Uninstall the App, or use your device settings (Apps → Topple Art → Storage → Clear data), to delete it immediately.

Data held by our service providers

Analytics and crash data are linked only to a random app instance ID, not to your identity. To request deletion, e-mail support@nberimen.com with the subject "Data deletion". We will process the request within 30 days. You can also reset your advertising ID in your device settings at any time.
