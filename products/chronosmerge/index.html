How to play

Jump right in — the rules are simple but the strategy runs deep.

1

Place a Tile

Tap an empty cell to place the next time unit from your queue onto the 6×6 grid.

2

Merge 3+

When 3 or more adjacent same-type tiles connect, they merge into the next tier.

3

Chain Cascades

The new merged tile checks for further matches — each cascade doubles your score!

4

Climb the Leaderboard

Compete for the highest score globally. Use skills wisely to keep the game going.
