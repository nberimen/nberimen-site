ChronosMerge Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

ChronosMerge ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the ChronosMerge mobile application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

When you use the App, we may collect the following information:

1.1 Gameplay Data (Stored Locally)
  • • Your high score, coin balance, and game settings are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you participate in the global leaderboard.
  • • No account registration, email, or personal credentials are required to play the game.
1.2 Leaderboard Data
  • • If you submit a score to the global leaderboard, your chosen display name (or a default anonymous name) and score are stored on our servers (Supabase).
  • • A device-based anonymous identifier may be used to associate your leaderboard entries.
1.3 Advertising Data
  • • The App uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads.
  • • AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address, and usage data for ad personalization purposes, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • • On iOS, the App requests your consent via the App Tracking Transparency prompt before using the IDFA for ad personalization.
1.4 Purchase Data
  • • In-app purchases (coin packs, Remove Ads) are processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, and managed via RevenueCat.
  • • We do not collect or store your payment information. All payment processing is handled by the app stores.
  • • RevenueCat may store anonymous purchase records and entitlement status to manage your subscriptions and purchases.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • • Save your game progress, settings, and scores locally on your device
  • • Display your score on the global leaderboard (if you choose to participate)
  • • Display advertisements to support the free-to-play model
  • • Process in-app purchases and manage entitlements (e.g., Remove Ads)
  • • Maintain and improve the App's functionality and performance

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third-party service providers to operate the App, including:

  • • Google AdMob — Advertising (banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads)
  • • RevenueCat — In-app purchase management (coin packs, Remove Ads entitlement)
  • • Supabase — Leaderboard data storage
  • • Apple App Store & Google Play — Payment processing

These providers process data only on our behalf and in accordance with applicable data protection standards. Each has its own privacy policy governing their data practices.

4. Data Storage & Retention

  • • Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App removes this data.
  • • Leaderboard entries are stored on our servers (Supabase) and may be retained for the lifetime of the service.
  • • Purchase data managed by RevenueCat is retained per their data retention policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

Your data may be shared only:

  • • With third-party service providers necessary to operate the App (as listed above)
  • • When required by law or legal process
  • • To protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the App

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. All data transmitted to our servers uses secure HTTPS connections. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • • Access your personal data
  • • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • • Opt out of personalized advertising (via your device settings)
  • • Withdraw consent where applicable

You may contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Children's Privacy

ChronosMerge is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by local law). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
