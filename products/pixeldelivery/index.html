Pixel Delivery

Delivery puzzle · Android & iOS

Every picture is made of colorful parcels. Tap a delivery truck to send it into the parking lot; its couriers pick up the parcels of their color and carry them to the depot. Plan your order, keep the parking lot clear and deliver the whole picture.

Features

  • •One-tap play: choose which truck goes next and watch the couriers do the rest
  • •Locked trucks, linked trucks, hidden colors, barriers and more mechanics that change how each level plays
  • •Helpful boosters: an extra parking spot, the valet, a shuffle and a delivery drone
  • •A speedy scooter for faster couriers, plus a daily gift
  • •Short levels that are easy to start and satisfying to clear
  • •Available in 15 languages; coming soon to Android and iOS
