Common questions

  • •How do I play? Tap a truck at the front of a lane to send it into the parking lot. Its couriers carry parcels of the same color from the picture to the depot, and the truck leaves when it is empty. Deliver every parcel to win.
  • •Why did the level end? The level ends when the parking lot is full or the road is blocked and no truck can move. You can continue with coins or an optional rewarded ad, or try again.
  • •How do lives work? Losing or leaving a level after the first levels costs one life. Lives refill over time; you can also refill them with coins or get one by watching an optional ad.
  • •I bought Remove Ads but still see ads. Open Settings → Restore purchases. Optional rewarded ads stay available by design.
  • •A purchase did not arrive. Make sure you are signed in to the same store account, then open Settings → Restore purchases. If it still does not appear, e-mail us with your order ID.
  • •I lost my progress. Progress is stored on the device; uninstalling or clearing app data deletes it.
  • •Which languages are supported? 15 languages; the App follows your device language and you can change it in Settings.
