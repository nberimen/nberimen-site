Odd Couples

Tile-matching puzzle · Android & iOS

Mahjong-style tile matching with a twist: tiles pair by meaning, not by looks. Bee & honey, lock & key, rain & umbrella — spot the connection, clear the board.

Features

  • •Layered tile boards; tap two tiles that belong together to clear them
  • •A 5-slot tray: pick freely, but plan ahead — a full tray ends the level
  • •Hundreds of generated levels, every one verified solvable
  • •Hint, undo and shuffle boosters; continue when the tray fills up
  • •One-handed portrait play, relaxing pace, no timers
  • •6 languages; early access on Android, iOS coming soon
