Cluster Blast

Block puzzle · Android & iOS

The block puzzle you know — but colors matter. Fit blocks on an 8×8 board, clear rows and columns, and watch the same-color group touching a cleared line blast in a chain.

Features

  • •Drag-and-drop block puzzle on an 8×8 board
  • •Cluster blasts: clear a line and the same-color group touching it bursts too, up to ×5
  • •Live preview shows exactly what will clear and blast before you drop
  • •Daily Challenge: everyone gets the same pieces each day, with streaks
  • •Bombs, rotations and tray shuffles; Jelly, Gummy and Crystal themes
  • •Colorblind mode with shape symbols; 6 languages
