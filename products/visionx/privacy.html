1. Information we use

Account

  • An anonymous account is created automatically when you first open the app. It is identified by a random user ID — no name, email or phone number is required.
  • If you choose to protect your account with an email address (optional, where available), we store that email to let you sign in again on a new device. We use it only for sign-in codes and essential account messages.

Content you create

  • Text prompts and the settings you choose (model, style, duration, quality).
  • Photos you select for editing, animation, restoration or merging. They are sent for processing only; we do not keep your original photos on our servers.
  • The images and videos generated for you.

Purchases and credits

  • Credit balance, credit usage history, purchased packages and subscription status.
  • Payments are processed by the App Store or Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details.

Device and technical data

  • Your app language and, if you allow notifications, a push notification token for your device.
  • Limited technical records needed to run the service securely, such as request times used for rate limiting and a one-way (irreversible) hash of your IP address used only to prevent abuse of promo codes.

2. How we use it

  • To create the images and videos you request and deliver them to you.
  • To manage your account, credits, purchases and subscriptions — including refunding credits automatically when a generation fails.
  • To keep the service safe: content moderation, fraud and abuse prevention, rate limiting.
  • To notify you when a creation is ready (only if you allow notifications).
  • To provide support and improve reliability. We do not use your content to train AI models.

3. AI processing & safety

  • Your prompt and selected photos are sent securely to our AI generation provider (fal.ai) solely to produce the requested result.
  • Prompts may be automatically translated to English by an AI language model before generation, to follow your instructions more accurately. Your original prompt is what we store.
  • To prevent illegal or harmful content, prompts and photos are checked by an automated moderation service (OpenAI Moderation API) before generation. Requests that violate our rules are blocked and no credits are charged.
  • These providers process data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and not for advertising.

5. Service providers

  • Supabase — authentication, database and server functions
  • fal.ai — AI image and video generation; AI prompt translation
  • OpenAI — automated content moderation
  • Cloudflare R2 — private storage of creations (30 days) and template media
  • RevenueCat, Apple App Store, Google Play — purchases and subscriptions
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications

These providers process data only on our behalf, under their own security and data-protection commitments. Some of them process data outside your country; where required, appropriate safeguards apply.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data and do not share it for advertising. We share data only with the service providers above to run the app, when required by law, or to protect the safety and rights of users and the service.

7. Notifications

The app asks for notification permission when you start your first creation. If you allow it, we use a device token to tell you when a creation is ready or failed (with credits refunded). You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

8. Security

All traffic uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Your creations in the cloud are private and can only be downloaded through short-lived signed links issued to your account. Database access is restricted with row-level security. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

9. Your rights & deletion

  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete account. This removes your account, job history, cloud copies of your creations and your push token immediately. Purchase records are anonymized.
  • Delete single creations: remove them from your gallery at any time. This removes them from your device and history; any cloud copy is deleted automatically within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, export or restrict the processing of your data. Contact us to make a request.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription — please cancel it in your store account.

More details: Account & data deletion.

10. Children

VisionX is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date; significant changes will also be announced in the app.
