VisionX Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: July 18, 2026

VisionX uses anonymous accounts — no email or personal information is required to use the App. This page explains how you can request deletion of your data.

Deleting Your Data

Since VisionX uses anonymous sign-in, you can delete all your data by uninstalling the App. When you uninstall, your local session is removed and your anonymous account becomes inaccessible.

When your data is deleted:

  • • Your anonymous account is deactivated
  • • You lose access to all generated videos and remaining credits

You can also request complete server-side data deletion by contacting us:

  • • Email: support@nberimen.com
  • • Subject: VisionX Data Deletion Request
  • • Please include any relevant details that can help us identify your account (e.g., purchase receipts)

What Data Is Deleted

Upon data deletion, the following applies:

  • • Account data (anonymous user ID, credit balance, usage history) is removed
  • • Generated videos stored on cloud storage are permanently deleted
  • • Text prompts and uploaded images associated with your account are removed
  • • Purchase-related data is automatically deleted within 90 days

Data Retention & Security

Some data may be temporarily retained only for:

  • • Legal or regulatory obligations
  • • Accounting and transaction verification
  • • Fraud prevention

All retained data is securely stored and permanently deleted once the required retention period ends.

Partial Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of specific data (e.g., individual videos or prompts) without deleting their entire account by contacting us via email.
