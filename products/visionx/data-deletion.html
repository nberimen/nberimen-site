Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: October 28, 2026

delete_forever

Delete your account in the app

  1. 1Open VisionX and go to the Profile tab.
  2. 2Tap Delete account.
  3. 3Type the confirmation word and confirm. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Note: uninstalling the app does not delete your data on our servers. Please use Delete account (or contact us) before uninstalling if you want your data removed.

Deleted immediately

  • Your account and anonymous user ID
  • Credit balance (remaining credits are forfeited)
  • Job history, prompts and settings
  • Cloud copies of your images and videos
  • Push notification tokens
  • Your subscriber record at our purchase provider (RevenueCat)
  • Images and videos saved in the app on this device

Kept, but no longer linked to you

  • Purchase and credit transaction records, anonymized, as required for accounting and fraud prevention
  • Short-lived security logs (up to 1 day)

Not affected

  • Files you saved to your phone's photo library
  • App Store / Google Play subscriptions — cancel them in your store account settings
mail

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app, email us with the subject "VisionX Data Deletion Request" and any details that help us find your account (for example, a purchase receipt or the account ID shown in Profile). We respond within 30 days.
